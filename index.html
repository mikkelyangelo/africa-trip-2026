    How to read the fares

    Source. Kayak. For traveller A only hubs that need no visa for a Russian passport: Istanbul, Doha, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Addis Ababa, Johannesburg. Traveller B has a French passport, so any hub works for B.

    Estimates. Kayak does not sell the FlyNamibia flights between Windhoek and Victoria Falls. They cost $160–230 in October–November; $250 is used for the holidays.

    Paying. Russian bank cards do not work abroad. Car hire in Namibia and South Africa needs a credit card in the driver's name for the deposit.

    Health. South Africa, Namibia and Zimbabwe are not yellow fever risk countries, so no certificate is needed on these routes. Victoria Falls and Kruger are malaria areas: start prophylaxis before arrival.
